# Project Instructions & Conventions

## Official System Title & Naming
- **Acronym**: Always and strictly use **PLSMS** (Printed License Search Management System).
- **Forbidden Term**: Never use the term "LRMS" or "License Record Management System".
- **Full Title**:
  - Always use **"Printed License Search Management System"** instead of "License Record Management System".
  - Always use **"Printed License Search Management System (PLSMS)"** instead of "License Record Management System (LRMS)".
- **First Login Welcome Message**:
  - *"Welcome to the PLSMS Command Center! Your login was successful. To ensure official government data security, please choose your personal confidential password."*

## UI & Dialog Box Design Rules
- **Shape & Aspect Ratio**: Always design and create dialog boxes, alert prompts, confirmation modals, and popup cards in a **wide horizontal rectangular shape** (never square or tall/cramped).
- **Horizontal Width Expansion**: Always provide **at least 50% more horizontal size** (e.g., minimum widths of `max-w-lg`, `max-w-xl`, `max-w-[540px]` to `max-w-2xl` instead of compact 360-400px squares), ensuring generous horizontal breathing room so that text, filenames, badges, and action buttons never feel cramped or prematurely broken across lines.
